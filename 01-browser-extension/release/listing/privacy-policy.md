# Markdrop Clipper — Privacy Policy

*Effective: 2026-07-20 · Dhiva Labs*

Markdrop Clipper converts web pages, selections, images and YouTube videos into Markdown files. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## Data we collect
**None.** Markdrop Clipper does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, or page content. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

## How your content is processed
- Page conversion happens **locally in your browser**. The content of the pages you convert never leaves your device.
- Converted Markdown is saved to your Downloads folder or copied to your clipboard, at your request only.
- Your settings (formatting preferences and an optional API endpoint) are stored in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`) and sync only through your own browser profile.

## Network requests
The extension makes network requests only in two user-triggered cases:
1. **YouTube conversion** — when you paste a YouTube link, the extension fetches that video's public page and caption track from youtube.com to build the transcript. Nothing is sent to YouTube other than the standard page request.
2. **Optional self-hosted API** — if (and only if) you configure a Conversion API endpoint in Options, "Convert link target" requests are sent to *your own* configured server. This is off by default.

## Changes
Any future change to this policy will be published at this same URL and reflected in the extension's listing before taking effect.
